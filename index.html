How to play

Simple controls. The game itself is not obligated to be simple.

Move & rotate

←→ move the block. ↑ rotates it. WASD works too.

Drop it

↓ moves down faster. SPACE hard-drops the piece and adds drop points.

Pause / start

P pauses. ENTER starts or resumes. Sound and music controls are in the dashboard.
